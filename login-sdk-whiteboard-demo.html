🛡️ Answers to Hard Meeting Questions

Q: Can it be made for .og domains instead of .eth?

YES! In fact, .og is the #1 PRIMARY focus from Day 1! "Sign In With Ethereum" (SIWE) only describes the cryptographic wallet standard used by MetaMask on EVM chains. The domain verified on-chain is our flagship .og on Polygon (Chain 137). Support for .eth (ENS) is intentionally deferred to Phase 8.

Q: What if the player sells the domain right after logging in?

ID tokens expire in 1 hour. On refresh, our backend checks the Polygon contract again. If transferred, the player's name reverts to their short wallet, but they keep their game progress (Decision D3).

Q: Does signing in cost gas or transaction fees?

Zero gas! EIP-4361 (SIWE) uses off-chain ECDSA signature math. The player never spends a penny.

Q: What if a player has no domain?

Decision D8 allows 'Wallet-Only' login, and shows a 'Get your name on Endless Domains' funnel link!
